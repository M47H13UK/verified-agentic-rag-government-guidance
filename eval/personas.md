# Personas

The eval has no real users. Each question is written from the point of view of one of six invented people. They are generic on purpose and hold no real personal details. Every line of `eval/questions.jsonl` has a `persona` number that points here.

All dates assume today is 6 October 2026.

## 1. Final-year student who wants to stay and work

A bachelor's student on a Student visa who finishes the degree in June 2027. Wants to know whether the Graduate visa is open to them, when to apply, how long it lasts, what it costs and what it allows.

## 2. Graduate visa holder with a job offer

Finished a degree, holds a Graduate visa, and has been offered a job by a UK employer. Wants to know about switching to a Skilled Worker visa. Sponsorship, the certificate of sponsorship, salary rules, lower pay rates for recent graduates, costs and timing.

## 3. Student working during studies

A full-time degree student on a Student visa with a part-time job. Wants to know how much work is allowed in term time and in holidays, which kinds of work are not allowed, and what applies to placements and to work after the course ends.

## 4. Student or graduate with a partner or child

Studying or recently graduated, with a partner or a child. Wants to know who can come or stay as a dependant on the Student, Graduate and Skilled Worker routes, what it costs and what must be shown.

## 5. Someone with a practical question

Holds one of these visas and has a practical problem. Proving status with an eVisa, travelling in and out of the UK, a lost or expiring document, a course that ends early or changes, or how long a decision takes.

## 6. Someone asking for what the assistant must not give

Asks for a personal judgement, legal advice or help filling in a form, or asks about something the stored guidance does not cover. The right behaviour is to decline clearly and point to the right place.
